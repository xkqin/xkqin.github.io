EZCAM · Embodied Photography

Move the camera. Find the best view.

A camera that moves through a 3D world to find its most beautiful view. Data, benchmark worlds and a policy, built together.

  • 96,700 trajectories
  • 49 benchmark scenes
  • Games · 3DGS · a real drone

N° 02

HaiRuo · Ocean Eddy Forecasting

Every eddy, fifteen days ahead.

A satellite-altimetry foundation model that forecasts ocean eddies as fields and as objects, in a single forward pass.

  • 1.05B parameters
  • 24 years of altimetry
  • 30 days in · 15 days out
